OnlyOrders describes its workflow as **"From first message to delivered order."** The product site lays out four steps that take a customer from a casual DM to a package on the way — all coordinated through chat. This article walks through that flow step by step.

Because this blog is published by the OnlyOrders team, we are describing the product from first-hand knowledge and keeping the mechanics consistent with what the product site states. The specific steps and wording below come from the OnlyOrders developer site.

## Step 1 — Messenger AI replies

Everything starts with the customer's message. The AI answers price, size, and stock questions in seconds, in the shop's tone. The site labels this step **Active** and notes that the assistant "answers price, size and stock questions in seconds, in your shop's tone — and hands off when it matters."

The "hands off" part is important: the AI handles the routine layer, and when a conversation needs a human — a custom request, a complaint, a delivery issue — it escalates cleanly so the seller stays in control.

## Step 2 — Checkout inside the chat

Once the customer has decided, the order is captured without leaving the messenger. The product site says customers "confirm the cart, address and payment without leaving Messenger."

This is the heart of conversational commerce: the checkout is a conversation rather than a separate web page. The AI collects the items, the delivery address, and the payment method — such as cash on delivery or a mobile payment — and confirms the order.

## Step 3 — One orders dashboard

Every chat order lands in a single board. The product site describes it as a "single board with status, notes and totals." From there, the seller sees all orders across channels in one place, can review details, and can manage fulfillment without searching through chat threads.

## Step 4 — Courier tracking, auto-shared

Finally, the seller books the courier in one tap, and tracking updates are sent back to the buyer's chat. The product site says: "Book the courier in one tap; tracking updates are sent back to the buyer's chat." This closes the loop — the customer gets delivery visibility in the same conversation where they ordered.

## Why the flow works

The value of this workflow is that it matches how customers already behave. A buyer who messages about a product wants a fast, consistent answer; a seller wants those messy chat threads turned into structured orders. By automating the repetitive "first response" layer and the order capture, OnlyOrders helps a small business run its store from one inbox without adding headcount.

It also maps to the four feature blocks the site advertises: instant replies, one inbox, chat-to-order, and auto posts — together covering engagement, conversion, fulfillment, and marketing.

## What to keep in mind

As with any automation tool, the human stays in charge. The AI handles the standard cases, and the seller reviews orders, steps in on complex requests, and makes the final calls. That division of labor is what makes chat automation practical for a small business.
