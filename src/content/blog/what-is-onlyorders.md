OnlyOrders is a conversational-commerce platform that turns direct messages and comments into confirmed orders. Its pitch is simple: **"Your orders, on autopilot."** The product's tagline and core promise are built around letting AI handle the repetitive parts of selling so a small business can run its store from a single inbox.

This article explains what OnlyOrders is, what it does, and how it fits into the broader trend of chat-first commerce. Because this site is published by the OnlyOrders team, we describe the product from first-hand knowledge, and we keep pricing and feature details consistent with what the product site states.

## What OnlyOrders is

OnlyOrders is described on its site as an **AI Commerce Autopilot**. The core pitch reads:

> "OnlyOrders turns DMs and comments into confirmed orders — smart replies, live catalog checks, payment verification and courier handoff, all from one inbox."

In practice, that means a seller connects their social channels — starting with a Facebook page — and the AI assists with the day-to-day conversations that lead to sales. The product is built for **conversational commerce**: selling directly inside chat, rather than only through a traditional storefront.

The site describes it as "The AI copilot for social sellers — from the first salaam to the five-star review." That reflects the target audience: sellers who already do business on Messenger, WhatsApp, Instagram, and similar channels.

## Core features

OnlyOrders groups its capabilities into four feature blocks:

- **Instant replies** — "AI answers customers 24/7."
- **One inbox** — "Every channel, zero tab chaos."
- **Chat to order** — "Receipts → courier handoff."
- **Auto posts** — "Drops published on schedule."

The hero sub-line sums up the scope: "Everything a chat-first store needs, in one place."

In other words, the platform spans the full arc of a sale: engaging the customer, answering questions, capturing the order, confirming payment, and handing off to a courier — all with the AI managing the mechanics.

## How it sits in the market

OnlyOrders is part of the wider movement of small businesses automating orders through messaging — a topic we have covered before. For sellers in markets like Bangladesh, where bKash and Nagad are the payment habits and Messenger is a primary storefront, a chat-order workflow aligns with how customers already buy.

The product's free tier is aimed at letting sellers try it without risk: the site advertises "Start selling free", "Free plan forever · Setup in minutes · Cancel anytime", and "FREE PLAN, NO CARD REQUIRED".
